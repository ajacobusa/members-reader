# Etsy Shop Return / Refund Policy

Paste the **"Policy text for your Etsy listing"** below into your Etsy shop's
Policies → Returns & Exchanges section, and a short version into each listing
description. It is written to:

- Make personalized items **final sale** (no buyer's-remorse refunds)
- Offer **free replacement** for genuine defects (Gelato covers these — $0 to you)
- Stay within **Etsy Purchase Protection** rules so you don't lose cases

> **Why not "no refunds ever"?** Etsy's Purchase Protection overrides shop
> policy for damaged / defective / not-as-described items — they will refund the
> buyer and charge you regardless of what your policy says. The policy below
> gives you the maximum real protection that Etsy actually allows: no cash
> refunds for remorse, free reprints for defects (which cost you nothing).

---

## Policy text for your Etsy listing

**Personalized & Made-to-Order — Please Read Before Purchasing**

Every item in my shop is custom-made and personalized just for you. Because each
piece is created specifically for your order, **all sales are final** — I cannot
accept returns, exchanges, or offer refunds for change of mind, incorrect
personalization details entered at checkout, or buyer's remorse.

**Your approval before printing:** For personalized orders, I will send you a
digital proof to review before anything is printed. Please check the names,
spelling, wording, and design carefully and reply with your approval. Nothing is
printed until you approve it — this ensures you receive exactly what you signed
off on.

**If your item arrives damaged or defective:** Your satisfaction matters. If your
order arrives damaged, has a printing error, or is not as described, please
message me within **48 hours of delivery with a clear photo** of the issue. I
will arrange a **free replacement** at no cost to you. (Photos are required so I
can resolve it quickly with my print partner.)

**Not covered:**
- Change of mind / no longer wanted
- Personalization details mistyped at checkout (please double-check before ordering)
- Minor color variation between screens and print (all monitors display color
  differently)
- Delays caused by the shipping carrier

Thank you for understanding — making each piece to order is what lets me keep
every design personal and high quality.

> A digital proof is sent for your approval before printing. Damaged or defective
> items are replaced free within 48 hours of delivery (photo required).

## How refunds actually break down (for your own reference)

| Situation | Who pays | Your out-of-pocket cost |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer "changed mind" / didn't like it | Final sale — you may decline | $0 (you decline) |
| Buyer mistyped the name at checkout | Final sale — you may decline | $0 (you decline) |
| Item arrived damaged / print error | Gelato reprints for you | ~$0 (free replacement) |
| Lost in shipping | Gelato/carrier claim | ~$0 (replacement) |
| Etsy sides with buyer in a case (rare with proof approval) | You refund, but Gelato covers defects | $0–$11 worst case |

**The customer-proof approval step is your strongest defense** — because the
buyer approved the exact design before printing, "this isn't what I wanted"
claims rarely succeed.

## Operational tips

- Always require a **photo** for damage claims — Gelato needs it to reimburse you.
- For a genuine defect, offer a **free reprint first** (costs you nothing) before
  any cash refund — most customers are happy with a replacement.
- A goodwill replacement that saves a 5-star review is almost always cheaper than
  the lost sales from a 1-star review. Weigh the ~$11 against your shop rating.
