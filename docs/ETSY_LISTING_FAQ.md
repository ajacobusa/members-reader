# Etsy Listing FAQ (Before You Buy)

Paste this near the bottom of each listing description. A clear FAQ sets
expectations, reduces "where is my order / why does it look different" messages,
and supports your final-sale policy by making everything transparent up front.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this item personalized?**
Yes! Many of my designs are made-to-order and personalized just for you. At
checkout, please enter the details exactly as you'd like them to appear (names,
spelling, occasion, message). I create your design from what you provide, so
please double-check before ordering.

**Do I get to see it before it's printed?**
Yes. For personalized orders I send you a **digital proof** to review and approve
before anything is printed. Please check the names, spelling, and wording
carefully and reply with your approval (or any changes). Nothing is printed until
you say it's perfect. ✅

**How long until I receive it?**
Each item is printed on demand after your proof is approved. Typical timing:
- Proof sent: within 24 hours of your order
- Production: 2–4 business days after you approve
- Shipping: varies by location (you'll get tracking)

Need it by a specific date? Message me **before** ordering and I'll let you know
if it's possible.

**Will the colors look exactly like my screen?**
Colors may vary slightly between screens and the final print — every monitor and
phone displays color a little differently, and this is normal for all printing.
I use high-quality 300 DPI printing for sharp, vivid results, but a small color
variation between screen and paper is not considered a defect.

**What sizes / products do you offer?**
See the listing's size dropdown. Posters, framed prints, and canvas may be
available depending on the design. Sizes are in inches; choose the one that fits
your space. (Standard frame sizes make framing easy.)

**Can I order this as a gift?**
Absolutely — these make wonderful gifts. If it's shipping directly to the
recipient, just enter their address. Message me if you'd like it to arrive by a
certain date.

**Can I make changes after ordering?**
Yes — before you approve the proof. Just reply to the proof with your requested
changes and I'll update it (no extra charge). Once you approve and it goes to
print, the design is final.

**What's your return policy?**
Because each item is custom-made and personalized, **all sales are final** — I
can't accept returns or refunds for change of mind or for personalization details
entered incorrectly at checkout. **However**, if your item arrives **damaged or
defective**, message me within **48 hours of delivery with a photo** and I'll
send a **free replacement** right away. Your happiness matters to me. 💛

**My order arrived damaged — what do I do?**
I'm so sorry! Please message me within 48 hours of delivery with a clear photo of
the damage and your order number. I'll arrange a free replacement as quickly as
possible.

**Do you offer custom designs not shown in your shop?**
Often, yes! Message me with what you have in mind (occasion, recipient, vibe,
scenery) and I'll let you know if I can create it for you.

## Tone notes (why this works)

- **Transparency lowers complaints.** Saying "colors may vary slightly" up front
  means a buyer can't later claim the slight variation is a defect.
- **The proof line reinforces final sale** — you're not hiding the no-refund
  policy, you're pairing it with "you approve before we print," which feels fair.
- **The 48-hour / photo requirement** is what lets you claim defects from Gelato.
- **Warm closing ("your happiness matters")** keeps your star rating healthy even
  while the policy is firm.
