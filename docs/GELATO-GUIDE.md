# Uploading Designs to Gelato + Etsy

## Step 1: Connect Gelato to Etsy
1. Log into gelato.com → create free account
2. Go to Stores → Add Store → choose Etsy
3. Approve permissions

## Step 2: Upload Each Design
1. In Gelato → Stores → your Etsy store → Add Product
2. Choose: Poster or Canvas
3. Select size matching your PNG (18×24, 16×20, etc.)
4. Upload your PNG from `Desktop\QuoteForge-Output\`
5. Preview the mockup — check text is readable
6. Set your price (suggested: $18–$35 for posters)
7. Click Publish to Etsy

## Step 3: Fill In Etsy Listing Details
Open `Desktop\QuoteForge-Output\etsy_listings.csv` — for each design:
- Copy the **title** → paste into Etsy listing title
- Copy the **tags** → paste one per tag box in Etsy (up to 13)
- Copy the **description** → paste into Etsy listing description

## Step 4: Order a Sample
Before promoting heavily, order one sample poster to verify:
- Print quality is sharp
- Colors are vibrant
- Text is fully readable
- Packaging is acceptable

## Step 5: Promote
- Pin your mockup photos on Pinterest
- Post mockup reels on Instagram and TikTok
- Use keywords: "wall art", "room decor", "gift", "inspirational print"
